Retainer Packages

The retainer agency fee.

Retainers run in 3–6 month terms (3 months minimum), reviewed and renewed thereafter. This is Brass HQ's fixed agency fee for managing the retainer — see the note below before reading it as a total price.

N.B. This is Brass HQ's retainer agency fee — our management and service fee for running the retainer. It is not the total cost of the items delivered under a package (press releases, TV/radio interviews, sponsored posts, etc.). Media platforms set and regularly change their own rates for these items, so we don't fix them here. Once you choose a package, we confirm the specific items and platforms with you, get their current rates, and share a full costing before any work begins. The agency fee itself is fixed and does not change.

Package Contents

What each package includes.

Deliverables per 3-month term. These are not separately priced — the specific items you choose are costed against current media platform rates. Add-on services outside a retainer's scope are billed à la carte.

Starter · Foundation

A light, steady editorial presence

  • 2 press releases
  • 1 TV interview
  • 1 opinion piece / sponsored post
  • 2 hrs strategy support / month
  • Monthly coverage report
  • 2–5 day turnaround

Growth · Momentum

For teams actively building visibility

  • 3 press releases
  • 1 TV interview
  • 1 radio interview
  • 1 op-ed / sponsored post
  • 2 hrs strategy support / month
  • Priority same-day placement
  • Monthly coverage report

Premium · Command

For leaders who need a full outsourced desk

  • At least 5 press releases
  • Weekly media placements
  • 2 features or op-eds / month
  • Executive media training (quarterly)
  • Crisis desk on standby
  • Dedicated account lead
