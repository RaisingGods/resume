Our Thinking

The Safe Influence Framework™

Visibility is the vehicle, not the destination. The Safe Influence Framework™ is how Brass HQ turns a media placement into lasting influence — the discipline behind every press release, profile and campaign we build.

Why It Exists

Coverage without trust is a wasted placement.

Plenty of organizations get covered. Far fewer are believed by the audiences reading that coverage. The difference is discipline, not luck.

Fifteen-plus years of building media relationships taught us that a placement chasing attention alone burns out fast: journalists stop returning calls, audiences grow sceptical, and reputations become fragile. The Safe Influence Framework™ replaces the volume-first playbook with a trust-first one, and asks a harder question of every release, interview and campaign we build: will this make the audience trust you more tomorrow than they do today?

Every Brass HQ engagement — from a single op-ed to a retainer editorial desk — is tested against the framework's six standards.

The Framework

Six standards. Every engagement.

The standards work together: remove one and the influence you build becomes either forgettable, fragile or unsafe.

Trusted

Trust is the currency of influence. We build it deliberately — through consistency between what an organization says, does and delivers — and protect it in every placement we secure.

Ethical

We use the psychology of persuasion to serve audiences, never to exploit them. Coverage that manipulates eventually collapses; coverage that respects people compounds.

Memorable

A story nobody remembers is a placement wasted. We design angles and narratives engineered to be retained — because influence only works if people recall you when it matters.

Credible

Every claim we help a client make must be one a journalist — and their audience — can verify. Credibility is what turns coverage into authority.

Sustainable

We build media relationships and reputations designed to compound over years, not spike for a news cycle — the same relationships that give our clients 24-hour turnaround today.

Human-centred

Behind every stakeholder map is a person who thinks, feels and decides. We design communication around real human behaviour — not a distribution list.

In Practice

From philosophy to placement.

The framework is not a poster on a wall. It is a working method with four movements that structure every engagement — including our ongoing desk for the Mastercard Foundation.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We map how you are currently covered and perceived — by media, stakeholders, partners and audiences — and where trust is strong, thin or absent.

  2. 02

    Position

    We define the story you can credibly own: the angle, the outlet and the audience that connects your milestone to their interests.

  3. 03

    Place

    We secure the placement — press release, op-ed, broadcast interview, activation — each one tested against the six standards before it goes out.

  4. 04

    Compound

    Influence is an asset. We track coverage, protect the relationship with the journalist and reinvest — so trust built this year makes next year's placement faster and cheaper.

Influence you can stand behind is influence that stands the test.
