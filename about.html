About Brass HQ

Built by a journalist. Run like a consultancy.

Brass HQ started as a creative agency — and at the core, still is one. What sets it apart is 15+ years of media relationships that took a career to build, now placed at the service of organizations doing work that matters.

Founder & President

Chizoba Ofoegbu

15+ years spanning broadcast journalism, PR, development communications, stakeholder engagement, business strategy and consumer psychology.

CO

A career that started in the newsroom — at NTA — and grew into a discipline for building trust between organizations and the audiences that decide their future.

Chizoba Ofoegbu founded Brass HQ on a conviction shaped by both sides of the media desk: as a broadcast journalist, she knows what makes a story newsroom-ready; as a strategist, she knows what makes a story worth telling in the first place.

That dual vantage point — editorial judgement plus strategic discipline — is what Brass HQ brings to every foundation, NGO, multilateral, corporate organization and government programme it supports. It's also why the Mastercard Foundation continues to work with Brass HQ as an ongoing editorial placement partner.

Brass HQ is based in Lagos, working across Nigeria and pan-African media, with a lean team of 5–7 built to move at newsroom speed.
